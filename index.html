3D explorer · Learning

Uptime

A system you can walk through.

How does a big app stay up? Uptime turns its architecture into a little city of 27 buildings. Follow a request across it, grow it from 100 users to 100 million, chat with the people who run it, and break things to see what users would feel.

Sleepy Planet

Shh. Earth is dreaming.

Cheeky stars keep tumbling toward a sleeping Earth. Launch rockets to pop them before she wakes. Fourteen nights, each with a new kind of star, and every pop plays a note in the lullaby and lights up a constellation.
