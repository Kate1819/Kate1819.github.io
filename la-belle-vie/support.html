Help

La Belle Vie · your luxury life in Paris

My game didn't save

The game saves by itself every few seconds and whenever you close it. Tap Continue on the title screen to carry on. Deleting the app deletes the saved game.

I bought All of Paris on another device

Open the phone in the game, go to Settings, tap All of Paris, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I can't hear anything

Check that your device isn't on silent and the volume is up. Music and voices can be turned on and off in the game's phone, under Settings.

Make the voices sound more natural

The characters use the voices built into your device. On iPhone and iPad, open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, choose English, and download an Enhanced or Premium voice. Do the same for French. The game picks the best one by itself.

Start again

Phone, Settings, Start a new life. This erases the saved game.
