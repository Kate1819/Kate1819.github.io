Privacy

La Belle Vie · last updated 23 November 2026

The short version: La Belle Vie does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game (your character's name and look, your home, your hotel and your progress) is saved only on your own iPhone, iPad or browser, so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data, deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no login and no email address.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No chat with real people. Every message, call and post in the game comes from made-up characters inside the game.
  • No camera, microphone, location or contacts. The in-game phone and camera are pretend.

Voices

Characters speak using the voices already built into your device. The words are turned into speech on your device and are not sent anywhere by the game.

Purchases

The app has one optional one-time purchase, "All of Paris". It is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. A grown-up has to answer a question before any purchase can start.

Children

La Belle Vie is made for children and families. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to share, sell or delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
